TITLE: PoolSmart — an intelligent, self-optimizing pool controller for Home Assistant

BODY:
Hi everyone,

I'd like to share a project I've been working on: PoolSmart, an intelligent swimming
pool controller for Home Assistant. It does not replace your pump/heater — ESPHome
measures, Home Assistant decides, and your existing equipment acts.

It runs a priority-based decision engine every 30 seconds that balances energy cost,
swim schedules, and equipment protection. What it can do out of the box:

  - Smart filtration: daily runtime computed from pool volume and measured flow
  - Heat pump control: plans around dynamic electricity prices and solar surplus
  - Solar optimization: heats with free surplus when available
  - Self-learning model: learns your pool's heat loss, heating rate, and COP
  - Water chemistry: pH readings become clear dosing instructions
  - Compressor protection: minimum off/run times always enforced
  - AI advisory: suggests settings, but applies nothing without your approval
  - Lovelace dashboards: a detailed three-tab view and a simple household page

Installation
------------
One-click via HACS (custom repository) or manually; full instructions and docs are
in the repository. A complete hardware + ESPHome guide is included (BOM, wiring,
voltage divider, probe calibration).

Links

- Repository: https://github.com/rickertie/Poolsmart

or override your safety systems on its own.
